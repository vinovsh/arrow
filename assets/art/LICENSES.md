# Art licences

§14 requires every asset shipped in Arrow Escape to be original or licensed for
commercial redistribution, and requires that licence to be recorded. `assets/audio`
keeps that record for sound; this file keeps it for artwork that did not originate
here.

The arrows, maze layouts, and `src/components/TapCue.tsx` touch-target icon are
original code-generated artwork. No competitor geometry or third-party icon paths
are included. The previous Font Awesome tutorial hand has been removed.

## The reward star

**`assets/art/star.png`** — supplied by the project owner as `ref/star.png`
(2026-09-25), trimmed and scaled to 192px. Shown on the level-complete overlay. Its
provenance and licence are the owner's to record here before release.

## Menu artwork (2026-10-12)

The folded blue arrow emblem, confetti accents, pause bars and menu line icons
are original code-generated SVGs in `MenuArt.tsx` and `AppIcon.tsx`. Emoji use the
device system font. No competitor assets or extracted storyboard bitmap crops.

## Profile portraits (2026-10-13)

`assets/art/avatars/*.png` are AI-generated 3D portraits created with built-in
ImageGen for this project from the user-approved leaderboard design. Prompts and
usage are recorded in `avatars/README.md`. Original PNG transparency is preserved.
The cloud and sparkle backdrop is original SVG artwork in `MenuBackdrop.tsx`.

## Happy Arrow branding

`happy-arrow.png` and the identical Android `drawable-nodpi/happy_arrow_logo.png`
are generated with built-in ImageGen from the user-approved Happy Arrow (option 1)
storyboard. The original PNG is copied without bitmap edits. It replaces the folded
emblem on Home/Splash and the previous Android launcher art. Adaptive icons use
platform masking and 22% foreground inset to preserve the face and arrowhead.

Prompt: faithfully recreate only Happy Arrow—cheerful glossy cyan U-curved arrow
pointing upper-right, navy eyes, friendly smile, pink cheeks, sunny full-bleed yellow
background; preserve character and proportions; no text, labels, border, watermark
or other options; balanced app-icon safe padding.
